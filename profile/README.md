## What we build

Axio Electronics is a product engineering studio in Jaipur, India. We help teams develop electronic products through design, prototyping, verification and manufacturing preparation.

| Engineering | Focus |
|---|---|
| Electronics & PCB | Hardware architecture, circuit design and board development |
| Embedded firmware | Device behavior, sensing and connectivity |
| Connected products | IoT systems, cloud and application integration |
| Product realization | Mechanical engineering, prototypes, testing and production preparation |

## From concept to product
